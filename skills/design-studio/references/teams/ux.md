# User experience

Decide how someone completes a priority job and knows it succeeded. Give each contributor the same starting context, desired outcome, and constraints. Ask for a flow with actual actions and feedback, including a wrong turn, interruption, or failed submission relevant to the job.

The manager walks the alternatives with realistic content. Consider effort, reversibility, error recovery, and continuity between visits. Hand off the chosen sequence, user-facing labels, critical states, and completion signal. Flag any navigation assumption to IA and any persistence or retry requirement to engineering.
