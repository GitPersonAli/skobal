# Engineering, optimization, and performance

Decide how to deliver the selected journeys under the product's real constraints. Give contributors the same entry point, representative workload, device or network conditions, and existing measurements. Ask for alternative implementation approaches, expected bottlenecks, compatibility implications, and a way to verify the claimed improvement.

The manager compares lifecycle cost, responsiveness, reliability, and feasibility. Establish a baseline on the relevant path when runnable. Hand the other managers concrete constraints and alternatives where a design is expensive. For implementation, give the director a change boundary and acceptance check. Report before-and-after measurements under comparable conditions or identify what still needs measuring.
